Why UULink

0.1 s

Fast, even when you’re out

Your phone connects straight to your Mac, with nothing in between. Even on mobile data, a round trip takes under 0.1 s,1 so your Mac responds the moment you tap. Messages are compressed before sending and use less than half the data.2

Zero lost

Messages survive a dropped connection

You can send with no signal; it goes out when you’re back online and runs only once. Output from while you were away shows up after you reconnect.

No hub

No central server, so it’s safer

UULink has no central server and no account to sign up for. Your code and conversations never sit on anyone’s server, so there’s no single place that could be breached to expose everyone’s data. Everything between your phone and your Mac is end-to-end encrypted. Only phones you’ve paired by QR code can connect, and your Mac doesn’t open any ports. Security details

Other features

  • Use the Claude Code you already haveYour account and subscription work as usual; there’s no separate API key to set up.
  • Pick up terminal sessions on your phoneClaude Code sessions you started in the Mac terminal show up on your phone, and you can continue them there.
  • Results sent to your phoneReports, screenshots and web pages Claude makes can be sent to your phone and opened right there.
  • All your Macs in one placeAdd your home and work Macs, and switch between projects and branches any time.
  • No network setupNo VPN to install and no network settings to change: scan a code and you’re connected. The proxy you already use keeps working.

Install the Mac and iPhone apps, scan the code to pair, and you’re set.
